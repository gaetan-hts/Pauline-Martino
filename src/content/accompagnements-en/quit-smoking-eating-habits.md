**Hypnosis** first helps you understand your emotional communication: what this behaviour expresses, which emotion it responds to, which need it fills. With a tie-release protocol, you then detach yourself from the substance, because that need now has another answer. It isn't about fighting the craving: it simply no longer has a reason to exist.

**NEMO** soothes the emotion behind the need, whether it is linked to a current situation or an older memory. Once the emotion has calmed, the behaviour loses its purpose.
