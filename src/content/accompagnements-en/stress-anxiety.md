**Hypnosis** helps you let go of what keeps your stress going: a situation, a person, a demand you place on yourself, a burden that isn't yours to carry. Once that tie is undone, the pressure eases and you have room to act differently.

**NEMO** is useful when your anxiety is linked to a specific situation or memory: an event at work, a conflict, a difficult period. The eye movements help soothe its emotional charge.
