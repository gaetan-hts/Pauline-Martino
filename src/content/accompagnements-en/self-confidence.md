**Hypnosis** is at the heart of this work. With tie-release protocols, you let go of other people's opinions, the judgements you have received and the labels others have given you, or that you have given yourself. You then regain access to your abilities, your own resources, your full potential.

**NEMO** comes in when a lack of confidence has its roots in difficult experiences: mockery, bullying, humiliation, failure, repeated criticism. By soothing these memories, we free what was holding you back.
