**NEMO** is the approach I favour here. We start from the emotion as you feel it today, and the eye movements reduce its intensity, often from the very first session. You don't need to go into every detail.

**Hypnosis** then frees you from the person, situation or belief that this emotion is still attached to. You keep what is useful to you and let go of the rest.
