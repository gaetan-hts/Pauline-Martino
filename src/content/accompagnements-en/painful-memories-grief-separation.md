**NEMO** is particularly recommended. You bring the memory to mind, without having to describe it in detail, then follow my wand with your eyes. Little by little, the memory loses its emotional charge: it stays in your memory, but it no longer expresses itself in the same way.

**Hypnosis** completes this work with a tie-release protocol. It undoes the painful bond that still connects you to a person, a relationship or an event, while keeping what matters to you. In grief, for example, you don't let go of the love, only of the suffering.
